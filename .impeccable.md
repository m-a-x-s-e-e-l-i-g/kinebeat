## Design Context

### Users

Creators who have a song and a collection of video clips but do not want to construct
an entire music edit by hand. They want an almost automatic first result, followed by a
small number of high-impact choices: lock what works, rearrange clips, regenerate what
does not, and export.

### Brand Personality

Exact, kinetic, and controlled. Kinebeat should feel like a specialist audiovisual
instrument that produces surprising imagery without making its workflow unpredictable.

### Aesthetic Direction

A near-black desktop workspace related to Chronophoto, with the preview and musical
timeline as the dominant objects. Instrument events should read as precise rhythmic
structure rather than colourful decoration. Avoid generic dashboards, neon nightclub
cliches, glass effects, and a crowded professional-NLE interface.

### Design Principles

1. A strong first edit should take one song, one footage import, and one generate action.
2. Locking and regeneration must feel completely safe and predictable.
3. Keep source separation automatic; expose musical meaning, not model complexity.
4. Reveal effect depth progressively through the selected instrument or timeline event.
5. Keep processing local, responsive, cancellable, and honest about preview quality.
